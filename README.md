# Biyahe Budget: A Commuter's Fare and Budget Tracker ✧

Biyahe Budget is a Java-based application designed to help commuters in Batangas calculate and manage their transportation expenses when riding jeepneys and tricycles. It allows users to calculate individual, duo, and group fares based on their destinations and passenger types.

The system also applies student discounts, generates fare receipts, saves trip details during program use, and helps users monitor their transportation budget.

Many commuters, especially students, have difficulty knowing the exact amount they need to pay when traveling. This becomes more challenging when several passengers ride together but get off at different destinations. Biyahe Budget aims to make fare calculations easier, help commuters avoid overpaying, and allow them to manage their daily or weekly transportation expenses.

✧ **Who Can Use Biyahe Budget?**

- **Students** – Calculate discounted fares and monitor their daily or weekly transportation expenses.
- **Regular Commuters** – Calculate regular fares for individual or group trips and manage their transportation budget.

---

### What Biyahe Budget Cannot Do? ݁₊ ⊹ . ݁˖ . ݁

While Biyahe Budget can calculate transportation fares, apply student discounts, and track expenses, it also has some limitations.

- It only supports jeepneys and tricycles. Other transportation options, such as buses, taxis, and ride-hailing services, are not included.
- It only covers transportation within Batangas.
- It only recognizes regular and student passengers. Senior citizen and PWD discounts are not included.
- It uses a simple fare calculation formula rather than the official fare matrix for every route.
- It does not provide maps, GPS tracking, or an online connection.
- It currently operates as a console-based application rather than a mobile application.
- Trip records are not permanently saved. Once the program is closed, the saved trip information will be lost.
